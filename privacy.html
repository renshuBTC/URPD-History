Privacy

Bitcoin Supply Chart · bitcoinsupplychart.com · last updated 26 September 2026

The site

Bitcoin Supply Chart is a static page. It has no accounts, sets no cookies, runs no analytics and shows no ads, and it collects no personal data.

  • Your browser loads the chart's data from bitview.space, the charting library (Plotly.js) from cdn.plot.ly and the page's font from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services see what a browser sends with a request, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.
  • Y-axes you pin with PIN Y-AXIS are kept in your own browser (its local storage). They never leave your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes them.
  • The site is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps its own logs of requests under GitHub's privacy statement.

The videos on YouTube

This site uses the YouTube API Services. Once a week the site's own build posts its two full-history videos (the left axis at the tallest bar so far, and at each day's own tallest bar) to the site's YouTube channel, renshuBTC, with an app called Bitcoin Supply Chart uploader. That app is used only by the site's owner, only to upload those videos to that one channel. It asks YouTube for permission to upload videos and for nothing else: it reads no one's data, including yours, and keeps nothing but that permission, stored as a secret in the site's build. You can see exactly what it does in the source code: tools/video/youtube.mjs.

The permission belongs to the channel's Google account, and can be withdrawn at any time on Google's security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Because neither the site nor the app stores anything about you, there is nothing of yours to delete.

Watching a video on YouTube is covered by YouTube's Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
